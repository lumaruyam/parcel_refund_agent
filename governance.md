## Session 2 — the cost of error

- **Legal or reputational risk, not just money:** the **wrong refusal**. The receipt is a statement the company is held to (Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024): a merchant can quote it, publish it, or contest it. A pattern of wrong refusals is also a churn risk on the merchants who ship most. **Owner: Head of Support.**
- **Money risk:** the **over-refund** (silent leakage), up to the cap (€25 standard, €500 premium) plus the label fee. **Owner: Finance (CFO).** Rollback figure: €630 of leakage per 1,000 damaged claims, or > 2% wrong refusals, in any week (`02_investment_memo.md` §3).

### The six-question data audit

| # | Question | Our answer | Source type | To check with |

| 3 | **Coverage** | 8 item categories, FR + EN, standard and premium cover, all 6 clauses. **Gaps:** real photos rarely show the item (mostly box exteriors); no envelopes or mailers; no other languages; no multi-item parcels | measured | — |
| 4 | **Rights** | Bitext: CDLA-Sharing-1.0 (data derived from it must be shared under the same licence if published). Roboflow sets: CC BY 4.0 (cite them; verify on each page at download). Kled: commercial licence, needed before any use. Classifier weights: MIT, but the Ultralytics library is AGPL-3.0 (production: enterprise licence or ONNX runtime). Production legal basis: Session 1 Q2. Retention: §Purpose below | analogy (licence pages) | DPO |
| 5 | **Leakage** | Test sets are never trained or tuned on. Test A uses another seed, held-out item words and a different paraphrase prompt. Test B excludes the two Roboflow sets the classifier was trained on, plus near-duplicates (perceptual hash). The prompt is frozen before test B is opened. Every look is logged in `evals/CHANGELOG.md`. **Between customers:** no production data in prompts, no few-shot examples from other merchants | measured | — |
| 6 | **Freshness** | Policy v1.1 (6 Nov 2026). Any policy change regenerates the labels and the fine-tune, and test A/B are re-run. Pinned versions: Phi-3 registry version, classifier commit, Gemini model id. A provider-side Gemini change triggers a re-run before L4 is re-enabled | measured | — |

- **Purpose limitation (GDPR art. 5):** deciding damaged-parcel refund claims and auditing those decisions. Photos and traces are kept **90 days**. Decisions and receipts are kept as accounting records (duration to confirm with Finance). Reviewer decisions are kept as labels for retraining.
- **Isolation between customers:** there is no retrieval index over other merchants' claims. Each call carries only the current claim. Records, queues and APIs are filtered by `merchant_id`.
- **Known gaps by segment:** content damage on real photos, French messages, premium cover (20% of data), fragile items without the option, envelopes.

### Trust level per step

| Step | Trust level | Why (cost of an error) |
|---|---|---|
| Intake + idempotency | L4 (rules) | Paying twice is the cheapest error to prevent: the claim id is unique |
| Classify photo (YOLO) | No autonomy: an input to the cross-check | Over-predicts "damaged" on creases; it can only stop L4, never trigger it |
| Describe photo (Gemini) | No autonomy: facts only | Can describe damage that is not there: measured on test B |
| Decide clause + receipt (Phi-3) | L4 only through the guardrail | A wrong clause → wrong amount; the rules recompute it |
| Refund ≤ €20, all checks pass | **L4** acts alone, a person samples 5% weekly | Bounded loss: at most €20 per error, caught by the sample |
| Refund €20–100, and every decline | **L2** drafts, a person approves | Over-refund up to €100; a wrong refusal loses a merchant |
| Refund > €100, need-evidence, any incoherence | **L1** suggests, a person decides | Premium caps up to €500 + fee; ambiguous evidence |
